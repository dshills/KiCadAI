# Frozen-source and visual review

Disposition: retain as an opt-in experimental drawing policy, not a complete readability solution or practical-board promotion. This is local self-review plus independent evidence checks, not an external Gemini/Prism review.

## Findings and limitations

1. **Readability blocker — global names and remote guide.** Net names such as `composition_net_003` and `SUPPORT_PARTICIPANT_CONTROLLER__MCU_PF2` remain electrically exact but do not communicate local intent efficiently. Connector meanings require consulting the detached reading guide. The guide's fixed-size blocks can split a wrapped connection across distant locations. No net alias or invented signal direction was introduced to hide this issue.
2. **Readability blocker — locality improves unevenly.** The controller's explicit support-parent mean distance improves 74.3 → 61.6 mm, but C7 is farther from U3 (112.3 → 139.1 mm), and controller C8 is farther from U2 (56.4 → 79.6 mm). Standalone C3 also regresses (45.7 → 79.6 mm). The full row-level measures are in [readability-metrics.json](readability-metrics.json). They are descriptive, post-run measures, not preregistered acceptance thresholds.
3. **Readability blocker — whitespace, topology and field association.** Block membership is clearer, but the regulator remains sparse; the controller spans an A2 sheet and has a long analog-to-controller wire. Native cardinal fields remain upright and collision-free under the existing audit, but some capacitor references sit between rows and the op-amp value is crowded beside a signal label. Block membership alone does not establish conventional circuit readability.
4. **Hardening follow-up — malformed support provenance for boundary roles.** In `applyFunctionalLayout`, an unresolved parent chain reaches the explicit-boundary-role fallback for connectors or rail flags. Production synthesis supplied valid parent chains in these examples, and their recorded parents validate. Nevertheless, the fallback should reject a known-but-unresolvable support parent instead of reclassifying that component as a boundary. This code-inspection edge case was identified after source freeze; no repair or additional evaluation was performed. Do not claim adversarial provenance authentication from these tests.
5. **Coverage boundary.** The new policy is enabled through `ArchitectureSimulationPlanResolver.FunctionalLayoutProfile = schematiclayout.FunctionalOwnershipV1`; it is not a new provider schema, CLI/UI default, or proof for arbitrary circuits. Flat, real-library examples were evaluated. Hierarchical functional pages, multi-active fragment locality, user-authored cross-block relations, robust local headings and broad naming policies remain unproven.
6. **Verification gap — race timeout.** The composition-lowering package exceeded the unchanged 12-minute race-test limit while running the pre-existing behavioral corpus's nonlinear simulation. The other two targeted packages passed, and vet passed. No race diagnostic appeared before timeout, but the overall race check failed and cannot certify that package. No retry, timeout extension or claim that the layout change caused the timeout is made.
