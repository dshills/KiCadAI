# Frozen-source and visual review

Disposition: retain ownership-v2 as an opt-in experimental locality improvement, not a complete schematic-readability or practical-board promotion. This is local self-review with independent evidence verification, not an external Gemini/Prism review.

## Results and remaining findings

1. **Ownership hardening addressed.** Lowering now rejects unknown support children/parents, conflicts between fragment and support ownership, duplicate parent records, and unresolvable/cyclic recorded parents before boundary-role fallback. Both ownership profiles exercise the real catalog-backed requests. Connector-specific failures and rail-boundary cases assert that rejection leaves the caller request unchanged. Ownership-v2 additionally validates allowed source categories and their parent/role consistency. This is structural consistency, not cryptographic proof of an arbitrary submitted fragment's identity; the native runner still relies on trusted in-process synthesis provenance.
2. **Decoupling locality target met, broader readability not met.** Regulator mean distance falls 67.21 → 33.02 mm and maximum 79.56 → 35.56 mm. Controller mean falls 89.03 → 35.92 mm and maximum 139.14 → 43.18 mm. All 11 measured decouplers improve. These are schematic symbol-origin distances, not pin-to-pin, wire-length or PCB measurements. The four regulator capacitors now surround their regulator; the controller's supply capacitors flank the MCU and the op-amp bypass is adjacent to its block. Details: [measurements](readability-metrics.json).
3. **Readability blocker — connection intent and topology.** Opaque `composition_net_*` and long `SUPPORT_PARTICIPANT_CONTROLLER__*` names remain exact. The radial placement objective knows ownership and clearance, not conventional input/output flow: output bypass C2/C5 is left of its regulator while synthesized input bypass C3/C8 is right. The netlist remains correct, but proximity alone does not make the drawing intuitive. Local power symbols, functional labels and conventional pin-aware routing are still needed under an explicitly preserved electrical contract.
4. **Readability blocker — detached guide, large pages and association.** Both examples retain the detached global guide; the controller guide spans three widely separated text blocks at the top of an A2 landscape sheet. A wrapped net entry continues into a different block. The regulator remains A4 landscape with much unused space. Neither has headings anchored beside the actual circuit blocks. Fields are upright and clear under the native audit, and capacitor references are less interleaved than before, but the op-amp value is near the resistor-row value and still merits better field association. Full readability remains 0/2.
5. **Secondary support regressions retained.** Controller reset capacitor C10 moves 43.18 → 50.80 mm from U3 and reset pull-up R3 moves 52.85 → 53.34 mm. SWD J5 improves 76.79 → 54.36 mm, and the explicit-support mean improves 61.62 → 45.42 mm. Do not generalize the decoupler non-regression result to every support part. The search is greedy in stable component-ID order; it does not optimize all family distances jointly.
6. **Coverage and compatibility boundary.** Existing ownership-v1 placement remains unchanged when the new locality flag is off. Known malformed parent provenance now fails closed for both profiles. Fixed-component requests retain the legacy placement path. Tests cover input permutation, unchanged input maps, explicit multi-active parents, chained support, concurrent shared inputs and ambiguous/cyclic fallback. Arbitrary hierarchical pages, user-authored cross-block constraints, search-exhaustion optimality and all font/rotation geometries remain unproven. The profile is enabled through `ArchitectureSimulationPlanResolver.FunctionalLayoutProfile`, not a new CLI/UI default or provider capability.
7. **Scoped race checks passed; broader gap remains explicit.** All short tests in schematiclayout (3.505 s) and schematicir (208.727 s), plus `TestFunctionalOwnership*` in compositionlowering (2.764 s), passed under the race detector. Their terminal outcomes are in [race-layout.execution.json](race-layout.execution.json) and [race-ownership.execution.json](race-ownership.execution.json). They do not rerun or certify the previously timed-out full compositionlowering race suite. No timeout was extended and no unrelated simulation test was weakened. Vet and lint also passed.
