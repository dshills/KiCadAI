## Limitations and follow-up

The held-out capability claim remains unproved. Provider schema acceptance was
tested locally, not against a live endpoint. The request-size regression covers
16 initial/correction forms, not arbitrary clarification-state growth. The
regulator source check assumes the declared capability/binding semantics and
does not establish physical causality or exhaustive natural-language fidelity.

Participant-specific measurements and a standalone regulated output without a
real downstream signal consumer remain representation gaps. Do not use these
offline regressions to promote practical-board support. The next work requires
a separately scoped representation design and, eventually, authorization for a
new frozen live evaluation. Historical sealed replay failure stays a failure.

Final automated check outcomes, including unsuccessful setup/test attempts, are
recorded separately. No unresolved finding in this review justifies widening
the approved scope or rewriting old evidence.
