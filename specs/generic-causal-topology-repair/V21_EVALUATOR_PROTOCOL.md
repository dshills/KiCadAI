# V21 Generic Causal Topology Completion and Bounded Repair Protocol

Status: frozen before public evaluation. This document does not authorize or
record a V21 public run.

V18, V19, and V20 are immutable historical boundaries. Their corpora,
evaluators, reports, manifests, admission evidence, promotion evidence, and
retirement evidence remain unchanged. V21 is a version-isolated successor that
reuses only the authenticated public 24-case V10 discovery corpus, the exact
V20 synthesis boundary, the exact V18 comparison boundary, and the immutable
legacy boundary.

Before execution, authenticate the V10 corpus, the V20 evaluator manifest and
generation-zero report, the V21 selected-population freeze, the V21 contract,
and the V21 evaluator manifest. Execute from a clean committed Git tree into
fresh report and working roots. The evaluator has no held-out key or blind-data
access surface.

V21 uses the frozen V17 serial transport, processes all 24 discovery cases in
manifest order, and evaluates each case exactly twice. V21 first executes the
exact V20 boundary. A V20 pass, unsafe result, invalid or infeasible
requirement, canceled run, or result outside the selected public topology
frontier is returned byte-for-byte.

The selected population contains eight public cases across five reporting
domains. Selection is based only on authenticated V20 public evidence. Its 47
`causal_topology_repair` leaves and nine `complete_topology` leaves are frontier
occurrences, not case counts. Eligibility is limited to V20 terminal
`causal_topology_repair` or `complete_topology` exhaustion for a selected case.

Before any V21 structural planning, the exact V20 request-level analysis,
model, and solver admission gate must pass. Admission provenance is retained
through every repaired candidate. Numerical evaluation of a selected repaired
graph uses the exact admitted V20 evaluation path. No model substitution,
topology-specific operation, circuit-family template, fixture identifier,
coordinate, hard-coded net, component allowlist, or expected solution is
permitted.

V21 derives typed structural obligations from only the behavior contract,
primitive terminal contracts, and current candidate graph. It applies generic
operations in canonical order, hashes and deduplicates every state, detects
ancestor cycles, accepts only strict structural improvement, and records full
parent/operation/budget provenance. The frozen planner limits are:

- maximum depth: 3
- maximum frontier width: 8
- maximum generated work: 48
- maximum retained states: 64
- maximum conservative graph size: 1,048,576 bytes
- evaluator worker count: 1

The same selected graph and ordered operations must result for every supported
worker count. Cancellation, absence of a generic operation, contradictory
paths, invalid terminal/domain/observation relationships, cycles or duplicate
states, and every frozen bound fail closed with typed diagnostics. A structural
certificate may expose a later admitted behavioral, model, solver, value, or
physical blocker; renaming a topology failure without such evidence does not
count as advancement.

Material improvement requires at least three selected public cases across at
least two reporting domains to pass or advance from topology exhaustion to a
strictly later, more specific blocker. V18 passes, V20 admission behavior,
safety outcomes, and all ineligible V20 JSON must not regress. Publication is
atomic and no-replace. The published report records aggregate outcomes, typed
frontier changes, affected cases and reporting domains advanced, bounded work
consumption, regressions, and newly exposed generic gaps.

If the material threshold or any preservation gate is not met, V21 remains
experimental and fails closed. No held-out evaluation or capability admission
is authorized by this protocol.
