# Closed-Loop Open-Set Capability Expansion V13 Addendum

V13 starts only from the permanent, unopened V12 generation-zero retirement.
It reuses the authenticated immutable V10 corpus and the frozen synthesis,
observation, promotion, and gate semantics. V12 partial checkpoints are
retirement evidence only and may not be resumed or used for capability
selection.

V13 corrects the remaining evaluator resource defect generically. The evaluator
runs exactly one case at a time, retains at most one complete synthesis replay,
and explicitly performs a full garbage collection and operating-system memory
release after each replay becomes unreachable. No circuit identity, coordinate,
role, domain, outcome, or held-out information may influence scheduling or
memory release.

All other fail-closed boundaries remain unchanged. A public baseline is
eligible only after all 24 discovery cases complete exactly two identical
replays and all required evidence gates. The discovery evaluator has no
held-out key access surface.
