# Closed-Loop Open-Set Capability Expansion V17 Addendum

V17 starts only from the permanent, unopened V16 generation-zero retirement.
It reuses the authenticated immutable V10 corpus and the frozen observation,
promotion, and gate semantics. V16 partial checkpoints are retirement evidence
only and may not be resumed or used for capability selection.

V17 corrects the remaining production resource defect generically. V16 already
streams repair, synthesis, and replay encodings, but the trusted transient
simulator can retain up to one million complete node/device observation points
for a single uniform event-aligned analysis. V17 leaves the legacy evaluator
unchanged and introduces an opt-in 100,000-step deterministic ceiling for the
open-set path. Timing-resolution and exact-event alignment are preserved when
they fit that ceiling; otherwise the same bounded-grid rule selects the finest
permitted uniform step.

Each completed V17 simulation hashes the complete canonical proof with the
streaming encoder, derives the assertion result from the complete proof, then
retains at most 256 uniformly spaced witness points including both endpoints.
Full proof hashes, assertion values, diagnoses, electrical interfaces, search
order, ranking, and budgets remain authoritative. Encoding errors fail closed.
No circuit identity, coordinate, role, domain, outcome, or held-out information
may influence the bounds or hashing.

V17 preserves every V16 search, value-trial, repair, ranking,
exactly-one-case-worker, exactly-one-live-replay, and post-replay memory-release
rule. Output schemas are unchanged; retained transient witnesses are a bounded
subset whose complete-proof hashes remain available. All other fail-closed
boundaries remain unchanged. A public baseline is
eligible only after all 24 discovery cases complete exactly two identical
replays and all required evidence gates. The discovery evaluator has no
held-out key access surface.
