observation, promotion, and gate semantics. V13 partial checkpoints are
retirement evidence only and may not be resumed or used for capability
selection.

trials retain only their compact catalog selections. Candidate graphs are
validated in the same deterministic order and materialized on demand only for
the trial being evaluated. No circuit identity, coordinate, role, domain,
outcome, or held-out information may influence materialization or release.

V14 also preserves V13's exactly-one-case-worker, exactly-one-live-replay, and
post-replay full garbage-collection rules. All other fail-closed boundaries
remain unchanged. A public baseline is eligible only after all 24 discovery
cases complete exactly two identical replays and all required evidence gates.
