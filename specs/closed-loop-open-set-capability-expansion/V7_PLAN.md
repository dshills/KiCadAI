# Closed-Loop Open-Set Capability Expansion V7 Plan

Status: freeze candidate

## Phase 0: Contract and adaptive selector freeze

- Commit the V7 addendum, plan, corpus rules, baseline protocol, selection
  policy, transition vocabulary, contract tests, and checksum manifest.
- Bind the V7 starting commit and every inherited evaluator, synthesis,
  physical, writer, routing, catalog, model, environment, and retirement
  commitment.
- Prove deterministic frontier construction, cohort retention and dropout,
  minimal bundle generation, ranking, semantic-tie failure, round ceilings,
  total ceilings, lineage admission, selected environment transitions, and
  held-out non-influence.
- Prohibit corpus authoring and synthesis before the freeze commit.

## Phase 1: Fresh three-author corpus freeze

- Give three isolated authors only the committed V7 public author packet and
  disjoint assignments.
- Receive each bundle in a separate quarantine unavailable to implementation
  and to the other authors.
- Validate strict schema, meaning, references, bounds, gates, provenance,
  quotas, diversity, cross-author uniqueness, and V1-V6 historical non-overlap
  without synthesis.
- Permit at most three outcome-neutral validator correction packets per author;
  fully revalidate each corrected bundle. If an original author bundle remains
  invalid, permit at most two sequential context-isolated replacement authors
  for that assignment; retire if the second replacement remains invalid.
- Encrypt held-out source with a fresh external 0600 key and atomically publish
  the canonical corpus and commitments.

## Phase 2: Generation-zero discovery baseline and selection

- Run all 18 discovery cases twice from isolated roots.
- Require byte-identical complete replay exports and two clean-root
  installed-KiCad promotions for every pass.
- Publish complete root frontiers, suppressed/candidate failure inventories,
  causal edges, transition classifications, aggregate evidence, and all bundle
  decisions.
- Freeze generation-zero rank one, its active cohort, exact members, complete
  generic plan, and input-frontier hash.

## Phase 3: Untouched held-out baseline seal

- After generation-zero selection is committed and before any implementation,
  evaluate all 18 held-out cases through the identical path in an isolated
  custodian context.
- Encrypt source and baseline payloads with separate external 0600 keys.
- Publish only ciphertext, commitments, counts, algorithm metadata, and a
  non-revealing boundary audit.

## Phase 4: Bounded adaptive discovery rounds

- For each admitted round, implement only the frozen selected generic bundle
  and prerequisites that pass the frozen compile/execution/shared-invariant,
  no-independent-effect, and exact-path-mapping checklist.
- Add representative, adversarial, boundary, replay, and fail-closed tests.
- Prism-review and remediate the exact staged diff; self-hash and commit its
  production and verification artifacts with capability mappings.
- Rerun discovery exactly once and publish one of:
  - final public admission at the first strict pass uplift;
  - an admitted next frontier with cohort, lineage, and progress proofs; or
  - permanent retirement.
- Continue only while the frozen round and total ceilings permit it.
- The three-round ceiling has full capacity for three three-atom, nine-member
  bundles; unused capacity does not authorize a fourth round.

## Phase 5: Public chain admission

- Require strict total discovery pass uplift and at least one newly passing
  active-cohort case.
- Require exact case sets, no pass regression, no unsafe-to-pass transition,
  deterministic replay, complete simulation and physical evidence, admitted
  lineage for every removed nonselected gap, and only seal-bound environment
  transitions.
- Freeze the ordered capability chain and final implementation seal.
- Retire on any failure; do not tune, retry, or open held-out material.

## Phase 6: One-time blind held-out final

- Complete a read-only preflight before decrypting any record. Permit at most two
  pre-reveal retries only with an audit proving that no authenticated plaintext
  or evaluation was produced. After reveal, permit at most two automatic
  resumes from encrypted hash-chained checkpoints only for signed
  infrastructure failures with no human result access, unchanged execution
  bindings, and no completed-case reevaluation; otherwise consume the final.
- Recheck a clean sealed tree, the ordered chain, and three distinct external
  0600 key paths.
- Begin one logical held-out source/baseline session in the isolated custodian;
  checkpoint recovery may continue that session but may not reopen or
  reevaluate completed records.
- Require strict pass uplift, the hidden ordered-chain causal boolean, exact
  case-set preservation, no regression, no unsafe-to-pass transition,
  deterministic replay, and complete installed-KiCad physical evidence.
- Atomically publish encrypted success evidence and only the aggregate fields
  enumerated by `V7_SELECTION_POLICY.json.held_out_disclosure`, or a
  non-revealing permanent failure audit with no outcome-derived aggregate.
- Prism-review only non-sensitive staged evidence, commit, and push. The
  committed clean-tree local harness and external-custodian attestations are
  authoritative; do not manually dispatch or wait for GitHub Actions.
