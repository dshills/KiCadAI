# V10 Effect-Exposure and Causal-Round Engine

Status: version-separated adapter and evidence boundary prepared with
synthetic, outcome-neutral tests. No real V10 frontier has been loaded or
evaluated.

V10 deliberately adopts the V9 complete effect-exposure, deterministic
ranking, and causal-successor semantics. The V10 adapter exposes a distinct
package boundary while its checksum manifest commits both the adapter and all
adopted V9 implementation and test bytes. Uncommitted predecessor drift cannot
silently change a frozen V10 run.

## Frozen bounds

The engine requires exactly 24 discovery cases and the six canonical gap
stages/categories. It permits at most two rounds, six cumulative atoms, 18
cumulative exact members, three atoms and nine exact members per round, and
four causal successors per selected path. Candidate support and advancement
each require at least two cases, two reporting domains, and two circuit roles.
Complete enumeration is bounded at all `2^24` active-case subsets and fails
closed on overflow.

## Selection and preservation

Candidate evidence distinguishes fully covered cases from the complete effect-
exposure surface. It commits selected path hashes, nonselected sibling path
hashes, and full hashes for every non-exposed case. Ranking orders unlock
count, reporting-domain diversity, circuit-role diversity, and safety weight
descending, followed by collateral exposure, sibling burden, atom count, and
member count ascending. Canonical identity breaks only a full semantic tie.

Exactly-once round evaluation mechanically rederives the selected candidate
and its exposure. Selected paths may disappear only through satisfaction or
advance through bounded, append-only, nonweaker same-anchor successors.
Siblings and non-exposed cases remain byte-identical. Satisfied obligations
cannot regress, baseline passes cannot regress, and unsafe cases cannot become
passes.

Public admission requires strict total pass uplift and a new pass from the
original active cohort. Otherwise, only a mechanically admissible diverse
advance within the frozen budget may continue; every other state fails closed
to retirement.

The engine consumes typed public frontier values only. It imports no V10
corpus publication, evaluator, synthesis, outcome, or held-out key path.
