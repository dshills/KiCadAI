Date: 2026-09-14. Reviewer: implementing Codex agent, not an independent reviewer
or electrical-certification authority. Scope: the experimental indexed intent
path, its evidence/qualification tooling and the unchanged two-family native
generation boundary. Decision: **offline candidate may proceed to exact-source
CI and, only after separate approval, one final live evaluation**. Do not enable
it as the default, merge the draft PR or claim the goal achieved yet.

## Verification relied on

The frozen [runtime record](RUNTIME-03.json) identifies the exact local manifest
and qualification receipt. All 11 required preparation processes reached an
observed successful terminal state. Their stdout, stderr and process records are
retained and hashed, not inferred from PID/file existence.

Recorded qualification includes 73 Node unit checks, affected Go short and race
tests with the pinned compiler, zero-issue full lint, and real-Go non-native
collector/scorer integration (15 passes, two disclosed optional native skips).
Two separate actual production-binary native runs cover those native boundaries.
The earlier complete 14-case/five-profile synthetic integration remains valid
for this identical production and test-binary pair; the new collector changes
only add qualification checking, not provider or native behavior. The reviewed
five-profile reference examples are reused unchanged.

The new qualification's historical audit passed with the same failed final-02
scores: 5/14 raw, 7/14 application and 5/14 complete. It authenticated all 203
publication files, 177 evidence files and five CI-addendum files without changing
any prior prompt, response, score, budget, workflow archive or native output.

## Explicit residual limits

- No live output has been evaluated under indexed-v3. Hand-authored transport
  fixtures prove plumbing and deterministic behavior, not AI accuracy.
- The corpus is the same 14 **known regression cases**, seen during development,
  not an independent holdout or a statistical reliability estimate.
- Local hashes and declared reviewer identity do not provide provider
  signatures, independent review or protection against a malicious same-user
  author who can rewrite all code and evidence. Unsynced crash-time bytes are
  not guaranteed to survive power loss.
- `typed-v2` remains the default and its historical acceptance remains failed.
  Experimental availability must not be advertised as a generally reliable
  unattended language workflow.
- The native design/exports are software-qualified only. Heater-off, operating
  limits, assembly orientation, firmware obligations and physical-performance
  caveats remain in the family contracts and previous electrical review.
  Fabrication, calibration and bench bring-up need separate authorization.
- The previous green GitHub workflows cover published `f8ac5099`, not these new
  candidate bytes. Exact-source CI must finish before live-readiness is claimed.

No release-blocking defect was found in the reviewed offline evidence path beyond
the disclosed unproven live semantic acceptance and pending exact-source CI.
This is not a guarantee that no other defect exists. Any substantive correction
to frozen execution/evaluation inputs requires a new immutable runtime identity,
not editing the existing qualification to claim it tested different code.
