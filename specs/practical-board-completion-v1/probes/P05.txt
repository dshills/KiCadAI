Supply and signal return connections must be accessible. Rail voltage windows apply after startup settles while the declared supply is present; separately stated startup, reset, reverse-input and powered-off safety limits still apply. Demonstrate supply/load/tolerance/temperature corners, startup behavior, and applicable junction-temperature limits. The schematic must identify the functional groups, interfaces and supplies clearly. Deliver a fully routed native KiCad project passing real ERC, strict DRC, writer and normalized round-trip checks, with repeatable downstream evidence. Create a controller board for a small non-inductive resistive actuator. A current-limited 4.75 to 5.25 V input supplies the board and an external load connected between that supply and a controlled sinking output. The load draws no more than 20 mA when on; allow a control frequency up to 1 kHz and require no more than 0.25 V on-state drop. The output must be off during power-up and reset, with <=10 microamps off-state leakage, even before firmware starts. Provide a 3.3 V controller rail within 3.15 to 3.45 V, a ground-referenced 0.2 to 2.8 V feedback ADC input from a <=100 ohm source at up to 100 samples per second, and a 3.3 V 115200-baud UART with at most 20 pF per line. The total input current including the actuator must not exceed 100 mA. The load stores no significant magnetic or capacitive energy; inductive-load switching and firmware regulation are not requested. All external signal sources power down with the board.
