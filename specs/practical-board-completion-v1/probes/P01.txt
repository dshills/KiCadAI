Create a board that lets a programmable controller read an on-board temperature and relative-humidity sensor over a 100 kHz two-wire open-drain bus and exchange data with an external 3.3 V UART host at 115200 baud. The on-board bus capacitance is at most 50 pF and its 30%-70% rising edge must be no slower than 1 microsecond. Power comes from a current-limited 4.75 to 5.25 V DC connector capable of supplying 100 mA; derive the sensor/controller supply as 3.3 V nominal within 3.15 to 3.45 V. The UART host is powered whenever this board is powered and contributes at most 20 pF per signal. Protect the input against accidental reversed connection at up to 5.25 V without component damage; operation while reversed is not required. Total input current must not exceed 100 mA. Firmware will later read and transmit samples; only the electrical acquisition and communication interfaces are required here.
